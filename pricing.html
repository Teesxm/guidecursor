FOR WORKPLACES

Workplace accessibility,built to deploy.

GuideCursor is designed for annual per-user workplace licensing. Deployment can be funded directly by employers or through relevant workplace-accessibility provisions, with specialist suppliers and trainers supporting rollout where needed.

02 · DEPLOYMENT

From evaluation
to supported rollout.

GuideCursor is intended to move from controlled evaluation into supported workplace rollout, with clear accessibility, training and compatibility requirements at each step.

1 · EVALUATE

Validate with intended users

Evaluate real workflows with people with low vision or blindness before expanding supported applications.

2 · DEPLOY

Run a supported workplace rollout

Start with a controlled user group, defined workflows and clear support ownership before broader organisational deployment.

3 · SCALE

Expand supported teams and workflows

Expand only where target verification, accessibility compatibility and support quality remain reliable.
