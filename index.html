01 / THE PROBLEM

Point-and-click is efficient.
Not equally accessible.

Blind usersoften navigate sequentially through controlsinstead of directly pointing
Low visioncan turn small controls into search problemseven when the task is simple
Motor controlcan make the last few pixels the hardest parttiny hit areas, high precision

GuideCursor is designed around the existing interface, not a replacement interface.

02 / THE SYSTEM

AI that stays
beside your cursor.

GuideCursor does not take over the screen. It reads the interface, understands the task and helps the user move through the same workflow themselves.

✓ Human intent stays in control

05 / RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Not a feature in search of a problem.

The concept combines ideas already supported across accessibility and HCI research: mouse-like access, screen understanding, larger effective targets and ability-based design.
