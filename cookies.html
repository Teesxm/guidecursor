1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text values a website can store in your browser. Websites can also use browser technologies such as local storage, which are not cookies but can serve similar preference or state-management purposes.

2. Categories used by this site

Essential

Essential storage supports the features you ask the website to provide, including remembering your consent choice and tracking acknowledged deployment updates.

Preferences

The preference centre allows you to opt into remembering optional experience choices. The current MVP does not store a sensitive accessibility profile when you toggle this category.

Analytics

The preference centre also includes an analytics category, but the current MVP does not load a third-party analytics SDK or advertising tracker. Enabling the category records your choice for future-compatible behaviour only.

4. How the consent experience is designed

Optional categories start off. The first-layer banner gives direct access to both “Essential only” and “Accept all”, with a separate Manage option. You can revisit the same controls at any time from the footer.

Why this matters. Under EU privacy rules, consent should be a genuine choice. Technically necessary storage can be used without consent, while non-essential storage generally requires prior consent.

5. Change or withdraw your choice

Open the preference centre below to change the optional categories. You can also remove cookies and local storage using your browser's site-data controls.
