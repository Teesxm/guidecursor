1. Scope

These terms apply to this GuideCursor website and its interactive concept demonstrations. GuideCursor is currently presented as a student MVP for evaluation and presentation purposes.

2. The product is a concept

Nothing on this site should be interpreted as a live commercial offer. Product capabilities, prices, plan names, accessibility features and platform support shown here are concept material and may change.

The site demonstrates how GuideCursor could work. It does not provide production assistive technology and should not be relied on for safety-critical or accessibility-critical tasks.

3. Checkout and payment simulations

Apple Pay, PayPal, card, invoice, purchase-order and bank-verification screens are simulations. They are not connected to Apple, PayPal, a card network, a bank or a payment processor. Completing a fake checkout creates only local demo state in your browser.

Please do not enter real card numbers, passwords or sensitive payment information into the demo.

4. Simulated downloads

The macOS and Windows download buttons generate tiny inert demo files. They are not installers, do not contain executable GuideCursor software and do not grant a production software licence.
