1. Scope

These terms apply to this GuideCursor website and its interactive concept demonstrations. GuideCursor is currently presented as a student MVP for evaluation and presentation purposes.

2. The product is a student MVP

Nothing on this site should be interpreted as a production assistive-technology release. The current scope is visual impairment on macOS, with one core Mail journey and limited stretch tests. Earlier hearing, motor and Windows concepts are outside the first version.

The browser site demonstrates the interaction concept. A separate native Swift prototype is under development and testing, but it is not yet a production accessibility product.

3. Commercial status

The website no longer presents a consumer checkout or fictional plan tiers. The pitch-paper business model is an annual workplace licence per user, intended to be funded by an employer or potentially through a UWV work provision. No commercial price has been set and nothing on this site creates a subscription, purchase or licence.

4. Prototype status

A native macOS prototype exists in the project repository and is being developed through a draft pull request. Build and test artefacts are development material, not a public production release. Windows is outside the first MVP.

5. Acceptable use

You may explore the public concept website, interact with the demo and use it for ordinary educational or evaluation purposes. You should not attempt to disrupt the site, bypass technical controls, impersonate another person, submit unlawful content or use the demo to collect other people's sensitive information.

6. Intellectual property

The GuideCursor name, interface concepts, visual design, demo interactions and original site content are presented as project materials. Third-party names, logos, research papers and external services remain the property of their respective owners.

7. Third-party links and references

The website can link to academic papers and external websites. Those services are independent of the GuideCursor MVP and their own terms and privacy notices apply after you leave this site.

8. Availability and disclaimer

The MVP is provided for demonstration, research and evaluation. Features may be incomplete, under active development or intentionally simulated. No warranty is made that the concept or current prototype is suitable for a particular accessibility need, workplace, device or workflow.

Accessibility note. A future production product would require formal accessibility testing, security review, platform permission design and appropriate user support before it could be relied on as assistive software.

9. Changes to these terms

These concept terms may change as the website evolves. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. A workplace pilot or production launch would require separate legal review of contractual terms, accessibility obligations, data protection, workplace deployment, pricing disclosures and applicable law.
