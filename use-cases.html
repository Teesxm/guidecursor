02 · WHERE IT COULD HELP

Six recurring situations.

The opportunity is not a specific app category. It is the recurring gap between knowing the task and successfully navigating the interface.

Work

Dense workplace software

Enterprise tools can contain crowded ribbons, nested menus and controls that move between screens.

BarrierFinding the exact control consistently.GuideCursor roleLocate, highlight and guide toward the intended action.
Education

Student portals & LMS

Submitting work can require navigating modules, due-date panels, upload dialogs and confirmation states.

BarrierMulti-step interfaces with low visual clarity.GuideCursor roleKeep the next relevant target obvious without changing the platform.
Public services

Government forms

Long digital forms can become difficult when validation, navigation and next-step controls are visually inconsistent.

BarrierKnowing where to continue after each field or error.GuideCursor roleSurface the next control and explain the interface state.
Banking & forms

High-stakes interfaces

Account and financial interfaces can be visually dense, making target identification stressful even when the intended task is simple.

BarrierDistinguishing the right action from nearby options.GuideCursor roleGuide only. Keep confirmation and submission with the user.
Creative

Tool-heavy applications

Creative and technical software often exposes many small controls, panels and icons at once.

BarrierPrecision plus remembering tool location.GuideCursor roleCombine semantic guidance with larger effective target zones.
Everyday

System settings

Basic device tasks can require navigating unfamiliar settings panels that change across operating-system versions.

BarrierFinding a setting without knowing its exact menu path.GuideCursor roleInterpret the request and guide through the visible interface.
GOOD FIT

When guidance is the missing layer.

✓The person knows the outcome they want but cannot easily locate the control.
✓The existing software already works, but its interface creates accessibility or precision friction.
✓The user benefits from visual, spoken, captioned or motor-assistance guidance while staying in control.
NOT THE POINT

Not another invisible autopilot.

×GuideCursor is not meant to secretly complete consequential actions while the person watches.
×It should not replace specialist assistive technology when a user needs capabilities beyond cursor guidance.
×It should not expand access or permissions beyond what the requested assistance requires.
